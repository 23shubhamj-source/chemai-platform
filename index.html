Chemical Safety & GHS Protocols

Safety Standards

Standard GHS hazard pictograms and risk mitigations for laboratory reagents:

  • ⚠ Flammable (H225): Store solvents (ether, acetone, ethanol) in certified fire safety cabinets.
  • ☠ Corrosive (H314): Concentrated acids (HCl, H₂SO₄) require chemical fume hood and nitrile gloves.
  • ⚡ Oxidizer (H272): Keep permanganates and nitrates strictly isolated from organic reducing agents.
